# Free-Tier and Local Options

Some supported providers offer limited free allowances, trials, no-auth endpoints or local OpenAI-compatible services. These are distinct arrangements, not a promise of unlimited/free-forever inference. Free-tier availability and terms can change, and local inference has hardware/operating costs.

## Configure deliberately

1. Inspect the provider definitions and current catalog in your installation.
2. Verify the provider's official eligibility, region, quota and permitted usage. A no-auth connection does not imply unrestricted or permanent access.
3. Connect via the supported OAuth/API flow or configure your own compatible endpoint. Do not reuse another product's credentials or model IDs.
4. Request an actually enabled model or add it to your chosen combo. This category does not have to be last in the order.

Kiro, Qwen, iFlow and hosted OpenCode services are upstream products, **not** alternate Kitsune names. Their historical model/plan lists are not current entitlement guarantees. Do not rewrite the genuine OpenCode Zen product/API identity when changing this gateway's branding.

## Limits

If quota is exhausted, respect the reset/retry guidance rather than rotating identities or proxies to evade provider policy. No zero-cost, nonstop-coding or universal Claude access claim is made. A listed model is not proof a particular account can call it.

No ByKitsune account is required. You control provider order and deployment. See [routing](/en/features/smart-routing/) and [usage](/en/features/quota-tracking/) for known/unknown quota and price behavior; unknown cost is not free.
